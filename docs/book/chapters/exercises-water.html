Exercises for Part III

Keywords

Quarto, reproducible publishing, coding assistants, Markdown, Python, EPUB, data analysis, k-means clustering

These exercises collect Part III’s hands-on work in order, one per checkpoint, as Part II’s did. Start the assistant in ~/water-quality/, next to ~/quarto-workshop/. The water checkpoints hold sources only, so each catch-up also copies examples/water/data/ into your folder if data/ is missing. From Exercise III.4 on, the normal route copies each block’s finished scripts/analysis.py; the prompts that write the analysis yourself are optional. Appendix E — Checkpoints at a Glance, and Answers has the whole table.

Exercise III.1: rules, environment and a first look

From 8  The Question, the Data and the Rules and 9  Look Before You Model. Copy the data, write AGENTS.md from the data’s README, create the uv environment, and build explore.qmd.

You're done when explore.html shows missing values by zone and IRCA by band, zone and year for 2010–2023.

Stuck for two minutes? Paste this into your assistant, then rejoin at the next exercise:

Catch me up: follow ../quarto-workshop/checkpoints/05-water-explore/README.md

Exercise III.8: change an input, explain the rebuild

From Change an input, explain the rebuild, with no prompt supplied. Change MAX_MISSING = 2 to 1 in scripts/analysis.py, predict which outputs change, rebuild, explain, and undo with git restore scripts/analysis.py.

You're done when you can name the outputs that changed, the ones that did not, and the mechanism that links them (pre-render, outputs/, freeze: false). The model answer is in Appendix E — Checkpoints at a Glance, and Answers.

Going further

  1. Optional pieces. Build the dashboard and the white paper (A dashboard and a white paper (optional)); the finished project, examples/water/, is their checkpoint:

    Stuck for two minutes? Paste this into your assistant, then rejoin at the next exercise:

    Catch me up: follow ../quarto-workshop/examples/water/README.md

  2. Change the seed. Repeat Exercise III.8 with SEED instead of MAX_MISSING. Clusters are numbered by their centre’s IRCA, so the names and colours should stay; check whether anything else moves, and why very little does (Choosing k, on stability).

  3. Freeze it. Switch the site to freeze: auto, change MAX_MISSING, render, and find a page that kept its old numbers. Then write the invalidation rule into the project’s AGENTS.md, apply it, and undo both changes.

  4. The challenges in Further challenges: moves between kinds as a Sankey diagram, positive deviants, and other clustering methods.
