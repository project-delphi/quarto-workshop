# Partner email

The email that carries the partner brief: copy the block into a new message to a partner
hotel and attach `london-email.docx`. It holds no numbers; the brief computes them from
`data/` and cites them.

```text
Subject: London visitor guide for your guests

Dear partner,

Please find attached our partner brief, london-email.docx, ready to
attach to your guests' welcome emails. It covers:

- Why London: 2025 visits to ALVA member attractions
- Five sights to start with, each with its official visitor page
- Getting around by Tube, bus and Santander Cycles

It is a Word file, so you can send it as it is or add your hotel's
details. Every figure in it carries its source.

Kind regards,

London Tourism Office
```
