# Partner email

The email that carries the partner brief. Copy the text between the fence lines: the
`Subject:` line into the subject field, the rest into the message. Then attach
`london-email.docx`. It quotes no visit numbers; the brief computes them from `data/` and
cites them.

```text
Subject: London visitor guide for your guests

Dear partner,

Attached is our partner brief, london-email.docx,
for your guests' welcome emails. It covers:

- Why London: visits to ALVA member attractions
- Five sights to start with, each with its official visitor page
- Getting around by Tube, bus and Santander Cycles

It is a Word file, so you can send it as it is or add your hotel's
details. Every figure in it carries its source.

London Tourism Office
```
